01 — Our Story

Some conversations just click.

Ours started at a family gathering over food where small talk quickly turned into something with real spark. Our humor clicked like it was already familiar, and every conversation after that only made it feel more right.

Life with us has been a mix of laughter, spontaneous plans, and figuring things out as we go — through the easy moments and the "we're fine…we're not fine" ones. It's not always perfect, but it's always real and always something that feels like us.
